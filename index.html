About SALU

Shah Abdul Latif University, Khairpur

Shah Abdul Latif University is a public university located in Khairpur, Sindh, Pakistan. The university provides higher education in different fields of science, arts, social sciences, business and technology.

The university focuses on education, research, innovation and the development of students and society.
